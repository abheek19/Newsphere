Our Story & Heritage

A Small Local Shop, Now Bringing Warmth to Every Home

Founded in the heart of the Artisan Quarter, Little Joys was born from a simple belief: daily objects should spark joy.

For years, our physical boutique has been the beloved sanctuary where neighborhood creators, homeowners, and gift seekers meet. By partnering with local master crafters and sustainable suppliers, we bring you timeless lifestyle pieces that elevate everyday routines.
